# Contributing to Git-Explain

First of all, thank you for your interest in Git-Explain! We are building a tool to make developer lives easier, and we are happy to have you on board.

## How to Contribute
We welcome contributions from everyone. Whether it's a bug fix, a new feature, or an improvement to the documentation, we want your help!

### The Process
1. **Fork the repository.**
2. **Create a branch** for your feature or fix.
3. **Make your changes** and test them thoroughly.
4. **Submit a Pull Request (PR).**

### Review Policy
I personally review every Pull Request. I will look at your code, test it, and either merge it or provide feedback on how to improve it. Don't worry if your PR isn't perfect on the first try — we will work together to get it merged.

## Become a Collaborator
I am looking for people who are passionate about this project and want to help it grow. 

If you make **approximately 3 successful, merged Pull Requests**, I will invite you to become a **Collaborator** on the project. This gives you direct access to push code, manage issues, and help shape the future of Git-Explain.

## Guidelines
* **Keep it clean:** Write clean, readable code.
* **Respect the style:** Follow the existing coding style in the scripts.
* **Test it:** Make sure your changes actually work before submitting.

Let's build something great together!
